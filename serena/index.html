How to Reach Us

📍
Open in Google Maps 43.267296, 11.9976595 — Cortona
1
Coming from the top of the hill

The entrance avenue of Villa Serena & Dépendance is on your left, just after Hotel Oasi.

2
Coming from the bottom of the hill

Do not turn immediately at the first entrance — it is on a dangerous curve. Continue uphill ~150m to Hotel Oasi Neumann's small parking on the right (blue arrow). Maneuver there, turn left back down the hill.

3
The entrance

After Hotel Oasi's large wall, the villa gate is on your left. You can pull into the driveway to park briefly while collecting the keys.

At the Gate — Key Collection

🗝️ Find your keys in the wall on the right of the gate
Take only the keys labelled
SERENA
Inside the hole in the wall to the right of the gate you will find the keys to your villa and the remote control that opens the gate automatically. Take only the keys marked SERENA. The gate closes automatically behind you.

Parking & Your Villa

🅿️
Car Park

After entering through the gate, the car park is on the right side of the private driveway.

🏡
Your Villa

Villa Serena is the main villa — the large white house you see first as you enter the property. Your key opens the main front door.

🚪
Interior Doors

Additional keys for interior doors are attached directly to those doors inside the villa.

Check-in Procedure

1
Confirm arrival via WhatsApp

As soon as you enter the villa, send us a message on WhatsApp confirming you have arrived and taken possession of the villa.

2
Complete the web check-in

Use the link we sent you by email to complete the online check-in form. Alternatively, send copies of all guests' passports or ID cards via WhatsApp.

3
Registration within 12 hours

Registration is mandatory by law and must be completed within the first 12 hours after check-in. Thank you for your cooperation!
