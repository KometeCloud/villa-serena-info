How to Reach Us

📍
Open in Google Maps 43.267296, 11.9976595 — Cortona
1
Coming from the top of the hill

The entrance avenue of Villa Serena & Dépendance is on your left, just after Hotel Oasi.

2
Coming from the bottom of the hill

Do not turn immediately at the first entrance — it is on a dangerous curve. Continue uphill ~150m to Hotel Oasi Neumann's small parking on the right. Maneuver there, turn left back down the hill.

3
The entrance

After Hotel Oasi's large wall, the villa gate is on your left. Pull into the driveway to collect your keys.

At the Gate — Key Collection

🗝️ Find your keys in the wall on the right of the gate
Take only the keys labelled
1357E
Inside the hole in the wall to the right of the gate you will find the keys to your apartment and the remote control that opens the gate automatically. Take only the keys marked SERENELLA. The gate closes automatically behind you.

Parking & Your Apartment

🅿️
Car Park

After entering through the gate, the car park is on the right side of the private driveway.

🏠
Your Apartment

Dépendance Serenella is a ground-floor apartment within the Villa Serena complex. The main entrance is marked with a red arrow on the property map. Entirely on one level — no stairs.

🌅
Direct Garden Access

Your apartment has direct access to the garden. Enjoy the breathtaking sunsets from your private outdoor space.

🚪
Interior Doors

Additional keys for interior doors are attached directly to those doors inside the apartment.

Check-in Procedure

1
Confirm arrival via WhatsApp

As soon as you enter the apartment, send us a message on WhatsApp confirming you have arrived and taken possession of Dépendance Serenella.

2
Complete the web check-in

Use the link we sent you by email to complete the online check-in form. Alternatively, send copies of all guests' passports or ID cards via WhatsApp.

3
Registration within 12 hours

Registration is mandatory by law and must be completed within the first 12 hours after check-in. Thank you!
